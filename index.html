WELCOME TO OXFORD

Transforming Lives Through Education

Oxford College of Engineering and Management is an institution of higher education located in Gaindakot, on the banks of the Narayani River in Nepal.

Established in 2000, the college is committed to providing a nurturing environment and education that prepares students for the future.
